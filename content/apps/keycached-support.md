KeyCached keeps your frequently used snippets a tap away — save the text you
paste all the time, then drop it into any app from a custom keyboard, without
retyping it. Everything stays on your device.

Need help? Email **bspears-dev.handiwork967@passmail.com**.

## Getting started

1. Open **KeyCached** and add the snippets you use often.
2. Enable the keyboard: **Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New
   Keyboard…** and choose **KeyCached**.
3. In any app, tap the globe/🌐 key to switch to the KeyCached keyboard, then tap
   a snippet to insert it.

## FAQ

**Does KeyCached need "Full Access"?**
No — inserting your saved snippets works without Full Access. If a specific
feature needs it, the app will tell you why before you enable it.

**Are my snippets private?**
Yes. Your snippets and settings are stored only on your device. Nothing is sent
to the developer, and the app has no accounts, analytics, ads, or tracking.

**How do I edit or delete a snippet?**
Open the app and edit or remove any snippet directly. Deleting the app removes
all locally stored data.

**The keyboard isn't showing up.**
Make sure you added it under Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards, then use
the 🌐 key to switch to it inside another app.
