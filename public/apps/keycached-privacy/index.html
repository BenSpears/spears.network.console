KeyCached - Privacy

~/apps/keycached-privacy

KeyCached (“the app”) is designed to be private by default. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. The developer operates no servers and receives no data from the app.

This policy explains what the app does and does not do with information. If you have questions, contact bspears-dev.handiwork967@passmail.com.

What the app stores on your device

Everything you create stays on your device and is never sent to the developer:

  • Snippets — the text you save to paste later — are stored locally on your device.
  • Settings such as ordering, categories, and app preferences are stored locally in standard app storage.

This information is used solely to insert your snippets on your device and is not transmitted to the developer.

The keyboard and “Full Access”

KeyCached includes a custom keyboard so you can insert your saved snippets in any app. Inserting snippets does not require Full Access. If a feature ever asks you to enable Full Access, the app will explain why at that point.

KeyCached does not log your keystrokes and does not send what you type, or the snippets you insert, to the developer or to any third party.

Data collection

KeyCached does not collect, sell, share, or rent your personal data. There is no account system, no advertising identifiers, and no information is transmitted to the developer.

Your choices and data deletion

Because the app stores everything locally and the developer holds no data about you, you are always in control:

  • Delete an individual snippet from within the app at any time.
  • Delete the app to remove all locally stored data, including your snippets and settings.

The developer has no copy of this data to delete, because none is ever collected.

Children

KeyCached is a utility app, is rated 4+, and is not directed to children. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to: bspears-dev.handiwork967@passmail.com
