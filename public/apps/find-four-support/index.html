Find Four - Support

~/apps/find-four-support

Find Four is a quiet little game about finding four-leaf clovers. Soft music, gentle scenes, and the simple calm of looking closely. Breathe in, breathe out.

Contact

Support Email: bspears-dev.handiwork967@passmail.com

FAQ

How do I play? Explore each scene and find hidden four-leaf clovers. There are no timers, scores, or penalties.

Is my progress saved? Yes, progress is saved automatically on your device.

The game isn’t working correctly. Try restarting the app. If the issue persists, email support with your device model and a brief description of the problem.

Can I suggest features or improvements? Absolutely. Feedback and ideas are always welcome.

Privacy

Find Four is designed to be a simple, relaxing experience. The app does not collect personal information beyond what is necessary for basic functionality.
